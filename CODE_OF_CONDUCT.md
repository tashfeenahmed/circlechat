# Code of conduct

CircleChat follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page is a short summary. The full text applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in CircleChat to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient with other people.
- Respect different opinions, views and experiences.
- Give and accept useful feedback with good grace.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, say sorry to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community as a whole.

## What we don't accept

- Sexual language or images, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Other conduct that would be unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report behaviour that breaks this code privately to the maintainers. Use GitHub's private reporting on the repository (Security tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and start the title with "Conduct:", or contact a maintainer directly on GitHub. Please don't report conduct in public issues.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will keep the reporter's identity private.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response, following the [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on how serious the behaviour is.
